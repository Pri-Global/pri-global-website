**Prepared by:** Corneliu Secrieri  
**For:** Cornell, Elther, Robbie & Keenan  
**Date:** June 2026  
**Competitors:** Microsoft Power BI · ThoughtSpot · Domo · Qlik Sense · Tableau

---

## Objective

Position PR1SM.AI as a cleaner, more executive-friendly alternative to legacy BI platforms — emphasizing **conversational AI, operational intelligence, and actionable insights** without rip-and-replace complexity.

**PR1SM.AI value proposition:** Transforms fragmented business data into operational intelligence through conversational AI, dashboards, reporting, and actionable insights — backed by PRI Global’s 28+ years of delivery.

---

## 1. What they do exceptionally well

### Microsoft Power BI
- Massive ecosystem tie-in (Microsoft 365, Azure, Teams, Copilot)
- Clear product tiers (Desktop, Service, Premium)
- Strong enterprise trust and Gartner/ analyst recognition
- Embedded analytics in everyday workflows

### ThoughtSpot
- Best-in-class **natural language / agentic analytics** positioning
- “Talk to your data” messaging with Spotter AI agents
- Strong executive story: live answers, no dashboard backlog
- Free trial + demo CTAs; industry-specific landing pages
- Embedded analytics for product teams (APIs, SDKs)

### Domo
- All-in-one cloud BI with strong **executive dashboard** storytelling
- Mobile-first, social/collaboration angle on data
- App-store model for connectors and pre-built solutions
- Bold visual marketing for C-suite audience

### Qlik Sense
- Associative engine differentiation (explore all data relationships)
- Hybrid and on-prem options for regulated industries
- Strong data literacy and enablement content
- Embedded analytics for ISVs

### Tableau (Salesforce)
- Gold standard for **visual storytelling** and viz gallery community
- Role-based paths: Analysts, Data Leaders, Business Leaders, Developers
- Agentic analytics push (Tableau Next, Agentforce)
- Customer stories with hard ROI (£250M value at JLR, 10K users at KeyBank)
- Free trial + Salesforce CRM integration story

## 2. What appears outdated or overly complex

| Competitor | Issues |
|------------|--------|
| Power BI | Steep learning curve for non-analysts; licensing complexity; Microsoft-only bias |
| ThoughtSpot | Heavy enterprise sales motion; feature density can overwhelm mid-market |
| Domo | Pricing opaque; can feel like “another platform” vs intelligence layer |
| Qlik | Technical positioning; less approachable for business users |
| Tableau | Dashboard sprawl; Salesforce bundle confusion; agentic features still maturing |

**Common pattern:** Great for analysts who build dashboards — weak for executives who need **instant answers** without a ticket queue.

## 4. Where PR1SM.AI can differentiate

1. **Intelligence layer, not another BI tool** — No rip-and-replace; sits on systems clients already use (PRI delivery story)
2. **Talent + Technology bundle** — PR1SM.AI sold with PRI Global staffing, pods, and managed services (competitors are software-only)
3. **Executive-first UX** — Plain-English queries, seconds not weeks; vs dashboard-building workflows
4. **Mid-market speed** — Deploy in weeks with PRI AI Pods; competitors often need long SI engagements
5. **Operational intelligence** — Focus on decisions (inventory, pipeline, margins) not just charts
6. **Human delivery** — PriVa on marketing site + real consultants behind implementation

**Combined PRI + PR1SM message:**  
*Talent + Technology + AI + Operational Intelligence — end-to-end, few companies offer this.*
