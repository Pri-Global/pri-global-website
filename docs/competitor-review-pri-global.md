## Objective

Create a cleaner, more modern, conversion-focused website than industry leaders — while leading with **28+ years of staffing, consulting, and managed services expertise**, plus PR1SM.AI and PRI AI Pods™.

---

## 1. What they do exceptionally well

### TEKsystems
- Outcome-led homepage (growth, transformation, AI — not just staffing)
- Hard trust metrics: 98% retention, 93% NPS, $7B+ revenue
- Services grouped by business outcomes (ROI, growth, customer experience)
- Partner ecosystem and annual **State of Digital Transformation** report

### Insight Global
- Strong emotional messaging: *“The right talent changes everything”*
- Quantified credibility: 2,000+ AI-enabled recruiters, 71 NPS, 51K+ placements
- Clear split: Staffing vs Professional Services (Evergreen)
- Client quotes with specific outcomes (roles filled in under a week)

### Apex Systems (Everforth)
- AI-enabled positioning: *“Built to Adapt”*
- Partner badges (Databricks, Microsoft)
- Simple expert-connect CTA + jobs path for candidates

### Kforce
- Clear mental model: **Talent → Team → Project → International**
- Deep industry vertical pages
- Third-party validation (Forbes, SIA, NPS stats)
- White paper library for SEO and lead gen

### Experis
- Fortune 500 trust + 75-country scale
- Case studies with hard metrics (e.g. 60% approval time reduction)
- Recognition wall (HRC, Everest, Ethisphere)

## 3. Ideas incorporated in PRI Global (June 2026)

| Idea | Status |
|------|--------|

| Outcome-based service paths | ✅ ServiceOutcomes on `/services` (Hire / Run / Transform) |
| Talk to an Expert CTA | ✅ Hero, Get Pricing, Talent Solutions |
| Metric-led testimonials | ✅ Added outcome labels on select testimonials |
| Technology partner section | ✅ TechnologyPartners on Services |
| AI Pods dedicated page | ✅ `/ai-services` |
| Solution Quiz, ROI Calculator, PriVa | ✅ Already live |
| Candidate + Client + Employee portals | ✅ Portal menu |

**Still recommended:** flagship annual report, deeper industry landing pages, more case study metrics on homepage.

## 4. Where PRI Global can differentiate

1. **Integrated AI story** — PR1SM.AI + PRI AI Pods + 28 years delivery (competitors lack a product layer)
2. **Conversion tooling** — Quiz, ROI calc, PriVa, Get Pricing vs generic contact forms
3. **Portal experience** — Three portals in one nav (rare in staffing)
4. **Site clarity** — Less banner noise and mega-menu overload than enterprise competitors
5. **Mid-market accessibility** — Free consultation, 30-min strategy calls, pods in weeks
6. **Talent + Technology + AI** — End-to-end story few competitors can match
